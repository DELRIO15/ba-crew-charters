Two ways to book

Yacht only, or all inclusive

Every yacht in the collection is quoted both ways, by the hour. Yacht-only covers the vessel, captain, crew and fuel. All-inclusive adds the bar, the food and the service.

    Yacht only

    Covers the vessel, captain and crew, fuel, port permits and marina fees. You bring your own food and drinks; there is no corkage.

    All inclusive

    Adds the domestic open bar, food prepared aboard, ice and service. The difference per hour is listed for every yacht above.

    Extra guests

    Each yacht includes a set number of guests and carries a maximum. Guests beyond the included count are charged per person, per charter.
